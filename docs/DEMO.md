
# FieldTwin — Demonstration and Submission Guide

**Version:** 1.0
**Project:** NASA Space Apps Challenge 2026
**Execution Plan:** FieldTwin v6.2
**Status:** Demonstration plan — application not yet deployed

## 1. Demonstration Objective

Demonstrate how FieldTwin identifies and evaluates Earth locations as potential analog field-testing sites for Mars rover mobility and navigation.

The demonstration must explain:

1. The Mars mission-testing objective.
2. The Earth analog search process.
3. The measured terrain comparisons.
4. The limitations of Earth–Mars analogs.
5. The proposed rover field test.

All scientific findings must be supported by verified evidence.

## 3. Demonstration Sequence

### Screen 1 — Mission

Explain:

- The selected Mars terrain reference.
- The rover mobility or navigation problem.
- Why Earth analog testing is useful.
- The NASA datasets supporting the investigation.

**Judge takeaway:**
FieldTwin addresses a specific engineering need rather than simply identifying landscapes that resemble Mars.

### Screen 2 — Search

Demonstrate:

- The documented Earth search region.
- Actual dataset coverage.
- Verified candidate locations.
- Search criteria and exclusions.
- Known analog and negative control.

**Judge takeaway:**
The search is transparent, bounded, and reproducible.

### Screen 3 — Comparison

Demonstrate:

- Actual Mars terrain measurements.
- Measured Earth candidate features.
- Slope, relief, and roughness comparisons.
- The interpretable baseline.
- Candidate rankings and uncertainty.
- Optional ML evaluation if validated.

**Judge takeaway:**
The recommendation is based on measured terrain properties, not visual similarity alone.

### Screen 4 — Transfer / Rover Test

Present:

- The supported candidate recommendation.
- The Analog Transfer Matrix.
- Properties that transfer.
- Properties that do not transfer.
- Properties that remain unknown.
- The proposed rover field-testing protocol.

**Judge takeaway:**
FieldTwin connects Earth terrain evidence to a practical Mars exploration testing objective while explaining the limits of the analogy.

## 7. Presentation Guidance

Use a clear scientific narrative:

**Mission need → Earth search → Measured evidence → Scientific limitations → Proposed rover test**

Avoid unnecessary technical complexity.

Every visualization should help judges understand a meaningful result.

Distinguish:

- Actual observations.
- Computed measurements.
- Scientific interpretations.
- Proposed experiments.
- Remaining uncertainty.
